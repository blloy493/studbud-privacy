StudBud Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 16, 2026
Version: 0.1.0 (beta)

StudBud is a Chrome extension that breaks assignments into steps and
gives study help through an on-screen avatar. It is operated by
Brody Lloy, based in Canada. This policy explains
what data StudBud collects, why, and who receives it.

Contact: brodylloy@gmail.com


1. SUMMARY

- You choose what to type or paste. StudBud does not read the web pages
  you visit.
- Assignment text and chat messages you submit are sent to our server and
  to OpenAI so an AI model can generate steps and help.
- We collect anonymous usage statistics tied to a random install ID, not
  to your name or account.
- Your email is collected only if you join the optional waitlist.
- We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.


2. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device:
- Your current plan: the assignment text you submitted, the generated
  steps, your progress, XP, and focus-timer state.
- Any text you type into step fields (for example, citation details).
- Your avatar's name and your settings (hidden avatar, extension on/off,
  list of sites where StudBud is turned off).
- Usage counters (for example, number of assignments analyzed, and your
  answers to beta survey questions).
- A randomly generated install ID (see section 4).
- A local log of "Coming Soon" buttons you clicked (up to 200 entries).

"Reset progress" in the extension popup clears your plan and progress. It
does not clear your avatar name, usage counters, install ID, or settings.
Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.

The extension reads the address (hostname) of the page it is running on
only to check it against your list of excluded sites. This check happens
on your device and the hostname is not sent anywhere.


3. DATA SENT TO OUR SERVER AND TO OPENAI

When you use the features below, the data listed is sent to our server
(hosted on Vercel) and forwarded to OpenAI's API to generate a response:

- Assignment breakdown: the assignment text you submit (up to 8,000
  characters), your answers to any clarifying questions, and, for
  worksheets, the text of an individual question when you expand it.
- "I'm stuck" help: the step you are working on, the type of help you
  selected, your assignment text (up to 4,000 characters), and earlier
  help already shown for that step.
- Avatar chat: the message you type (up to 500 characters), recent
  messages from the same conversation, your avatar's name, and details of
  your current step (assignment title, step text, step number).
  Messages are also sent to OpenAI's moderation service for safety.

This data is used only to produce the response you requested. Please do
not paste information you would not want processed by an AI service,
such as passwords, government ID numbers, or other people's personal
information.

OpenAI processes this data under its own API terms and privacy policy
(https://openai.com/policies).

We do not intentionally store the text you submit. However, if a request
fails, our hosting provider's error logs may contain error messages or a
snippet of the model's output, which can relate to your assignment.
These logs are used only for debugging and are subject to the retention
period in section 7.


4. ANONYMOUS USAGE DATA

StudBud generates a random install ID the first time you use it. It is
not linked to your name, email, or Google account. We use it to:
- Count usage events: for example, plan created, step completed, plan
  completed, beta limit reached, survey answers, waitlist joined, and the
  time taken to complete steps or plans.
- Apply daily usage limits per install.
- Count, without any content, how often help is requested at each level
  and how often you flag a worked example as wrong. These counters are
  aggregated by help level and assignment type.
- Count interest in upcoming features, including which integrations
  (such as Canvas or Google Calendar) you ticked.

Usage events never include the text of your assignments, steps, or chat
messages. Because the install ID is random, we cannot tell who you are
from it.


5. FEEDBACK AND SURVEY RESPONSES

If you answer StudBud's feedback card or beta survey, we store your
answers (yes/no, price range, and any free-text comment of up to 500
characters) with your install ID. We also store non-content context
about your use: what prompted the card (finishing a plan or reaching the
beta limit), assignment type, number of steps, number of steps
completed, counts of plans, analyses, and avatar messages, and how many
times the card has been shown. We read free-text feedback. Do not
include personal information in it.


6. WAITLIST (OPTIONAL)

If you join the unlimited-access waitlist, we collect your email
address and the date and time you signed up. These are stored
separately from your usage data and are not stored with your install
ID. We separately record an anonymous "waitlist joined" event for your
install ID, which contains no email. We use your email only to contact
you about StudBud access and pricing. You can ask us to delete it at any
time (see section 10).


7. SECURITY AND ABUSE PREVENTION

To prevent abuse and control costs, our server applies daily request
limits per install, per network (IP address), and across all users.

- Install limits: a counter keyed to your install ID and the date.
- Network limits: your IP address is converted to a one-way hash on our
  server, and only that hash (a shortened code) is used for a counter
  keyed to the date. We do not store your raw IP address in our database.
  A hash of an IP address is pseudonymous, not fully anonymous.
- All these counters are deleted automatically after 48 hours.
- Our server checks that requests come from the StudBud extension. If a
  request comes from another origin, we log that origin (the website or
  extension address, not your IP) in our server logs.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) also processes standard request metadata,
including IP addresses, to deliver the service and in its own logs,
under its own privacy policy (https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
Data is stored in Vercel until deletion is requested.

Data in transit to our server is encrypted over HTTPS. Access to our
database and API keys is restricted to the operator.


8. WHAT WE DO NOT DO

- We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
- We do not read the content of the pages you visit, and we do not
  collect your browsing history.
- We do not use your data for credit, lending, or insurance decisions.
- We do not allow humans to read your data except where needed for
  security, to comply with law, or to read feedback you submit.


9. EXTENSION PERMISSIONS

StudBud requests only the following:
- "storage": to save your plan, progress, and settings on your device.
- Access to our server (studbud-two.vercel.app): to send the requests
  described in section 3 and the anonymous data in sections 4 to 6.
- A content script that runs on all websites ("all URLs"): this exists
  only so the avatar and task widget can appear on whatever page you are
  studying on. The script does not read, collect, or transmit page
  content, form fields, or your browsing history. You can turn StudBud
  off on specific sites or everywhere from the extension popup.
- Its image files are accessible to web pages so the avatar can display.

StudBud does not request permissions for tabs, history, cookies,
bookmarks, downloads, or screen capture.


10. RETENTION, ACCESS, AND DELETION

- Daily limit counters (section 7) expire automatically after 48 hours.
- Anonymous usage data and feedback are kept for up to 24 months.
- Waitlist emails are kept until you ask for deletion or the waitlist is
  closed.
- Anonymous usage data is tied only to a random ID that is not shown to
  you, so we cannot locate or delete records for a specific person.
  Uninstalling StudBud stops all collection.

To request access to or deletion of your waitlist email, or to ask any
privacy question, email brodylloy@gmail.com. Canadian residents may also
have rights under applicable privacy law, including the right to
complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.


11. AGE

StudBud is intended for university and college students. It is not
directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their
data.


12. CHANGES

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and
material changes will be noted in the extension or on this page.
