\f0\fs24 \cf0 StudBud Privacy Policy\
Effective date: October 16, 2026\
\
StudBud is a Chrome extension that helps students break assignments into\
steps. This policy explains what data it collects and how it is used.\
\
1. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE\
Your progress, XP, avatar name, settings, site-exclusion list, and current\
task plan are stored locally in your browser (Chrome extension storage).\
Resetting progress in the extension clears this local data.\
\
2. WHAT IS SENT TO OUR SERVER\
When you use these features, the text you type or paste is sent to our\
server (hosted on Vercel) and forwarded to OpenAI to generate a response:\
- Assignment breakdown: the assignment text you submit.\
- "I'm stuck" help: what you tell the avatar you are stuck on.\
- Avatar chat: the messages you send to the avatar.\
OpenAI's moderation service may also review chat messages for safety.\
[CONFIRM: whether this text is logged or stored on our server. Current\
statement: "We do not store the text you submit beyond processing your\
request." Vercel function logs may retain request data for a limited time.]\
OpenAI processes this text under its API terms. [CONFIRM: OpenAI states\
API data is not used to train its models by default and may be retained\
up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. Verify the current terms.]\
\
3. ANONYMOUS USAGE DATA\
We collect anonymous usage events tied to a randomly generated install ID\
(not your name, email, or account). Examples: which features are used,\
number of steps completed, active days, and plan durations. This never\
includes the content of your assignments or chats.\
\
4. FEEDBACK AND SURVEY RESPONSES\
If you choose to answer feedback or pricing questions, or click\
"Coming Soon" buttons, your answers are stored with your anonymous install\
ID. Free-text feedback is stored as you write it, so please do not include\
personal information.\
\
5. WAITLIST (OPTIONAL)\
If you join the unlimited-access waitlist, we collect your email address.\
It is stored separately from your usage data and is not linked to your\
install ID. We use it only to notify you about StudBud access and pricing.\
You can ask us to remove it at any time (see Contact).\
\
6. SECURITY MEASURES\
To prevent abuse and control costs, we apply per-user and per-IP usage\
limits. [CONFIRM: whether IP addresses are stored, and for how long.]\
\
7. WHAT WE DO NOT DO\
- We do not sell your data.\
- We do not use your data for advertising.\
- We do not read or collect the content of the web pages you visit.\
  [CONFIRM: true for the current build.]\
- We do not collect your browsing history.\
\
8. RETENTION AND DELETION\
Anonymous usage data and feedback are kept [CONFIRM: period, e.g. up to\
24 months]. Because this data is tied only to a random ID, we cannot\
identify which records are yours. Waitlist emails are deleted on request\
or when the waitlist is closed. To request deletion, email us at the\
address below.\
\
9. AGE\
StudBud is intended for university students (18+ or the age of majority\
in your region). [CONFIRM: this matches your target.]\
